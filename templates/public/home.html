What's Included & Verified

Real results,
watch us work.

Every end-of-lease clean covers 100% of your real estate checklist with proven before & after standards.

BEFORE AFTER

Kitchen

  • Dust and clean the exhaust fan and the extractor
  • Clean and polish all the countertops
  • Wipe the stovetop and rangehood
  • Deep clean the oven inside and out, including oven racks and trays
  • Clean all cupboards and drawers inside & out
  • Clean and polish tapware
  • Clean and polish splashback and wall tiles
  • Clean and wipe the sink and drain
BEFORE AFTER

Bathroom

  • Clean and sanitize shower screens, tiles & grout
  • Clean, descale and disinfect toilet bowl & seat
  • Wipe down vanity, mirrors and medicine cabinets
  • Scrub bathtub and clean basin tapware
  • Clear hair and debris from floor and drain grates
  • Clean and sanitize exhaust fan & air vent
  • Polish all chrome fixtures & glass surfaces
  • Mop and disinfect all bathroom floor tiles
BEFORE AFTER

Living & Dining

  • Deep steam clean and sanitize carpets
  • Wipe skirting boards, doors, and architraves
  • Clean switchboards, power points & door handles
  • Dust ceiling fans, light fittings & remove cobwebs
  • Clean sliding door glass & vacuum track debris
  • Wipe down internal window sills and frames
  • Spot clean wall marks, scuffs & high-touch areas
  • Vacuum and mop all hard flooring thoroughly
BEFORE AFTER

Bedrooms

  • Clean wardrobe mirrors, shelves & drawers inside/out
  • Wipe down wardrobe tracks and hanging rails
  • Dust and wipe skirting boards and architraves
  • Clean internal window panes, frames and sills
  • Dust ceiling cornices, corners and remove cobwebs
  • Wipe light switches, power points and door handles
  • Vacuum carpet edges or mop timber floorboards
  • Clean air conditioning vent covers & filters
