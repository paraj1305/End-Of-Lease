2. Definitions

Standard condition — a property in a reasonably maintained state consistent with normal wear and tear, requiring routine cleaning only, and not requiring restoration or the removal of built-up grime, grease, staining or marks.

Heavy-duty cleaning — cleaning required beyond standard condition. Examples include heavy grease build-up on a stove, oven or rangehood; soap scum, mould or hard-water staining in a bathroom; and marked or scuffed walls.

Estimated price range — the indicative price range shown by our online calculator, based on the details you enter.

Final quote — the quote we confirm with you after inspecting your property condition.

Deposit — the $49 booking deposit.

3. The quote and pricing model

3.1 Estimated range first. Our online calculator gives you an estimated price range based on the information you provide. This is an estimate only. It is not a binding offer and it is not your final price.

3.2 Callback and quote. After you submit your name and mobile number, we will call you back — we aim to do so within 15 minutes during our operating hours — to confirm the scope of work and give you a quote. The quote is the price that applies to your booking.

3.3 GST. All prices we quote, including the estimated range, the final quote, any add-ons and any surcharge, are exclusive of GST and are stated as a single total price.

3.4 Standard condition assumption. Your final quote covers a property in standard condition. It assumes the property is empty of belongings and requires routine end-of-lease cleaning only.

3.5 Heavy-duty surcharge. If, when our cleaner arrives and inspects the property, the property requires heavy-duty cleaning, an additional charge may apply. Before we carry out any additional work or charge anything extra, we will:

  • (a) identify the specific areas requiring heavy-duty cleaning;
  • (b) take and show you photographs of those areas;
  • (c) tell you the additional cost as a single GST-exclusive figure; and
  • (d) only proceed once you have approved the additional charge.

Nothing will appear on your invoice that you have not approved in advance. If you do not approve the additional charge, clause 12 applies.

3.6 Add-ons. Optional add-ons — such as carpet steam cleaning, inside fridge, garage and balcony — are quoted separately and are only included if you request them.

7. What a standard clean includes

7.1 A standard end-of-lease clean is carried out against a standard vacate cleaning checklist and typically includes:

  • Kitchen — benchtops, cupboards inside and out, stovetop, oven, rangehood and sink
  • Bathrooms and toilet — tiles, shower, bath, vanity, mirrors and fittings
  • Bedrooms and living areas — skirting boards, window sills, light switches, fittings, and internal windows where safely reachable
  • Floors — vacuumed and mopped throughout

7.2 A full room-by-room checklist is available on request. The specific inclusions for your booking will be confirmed in your final quote.

8.1 Unless separately quoted and agreed, a standard clean does not include: carpet steam cleaning; inside the fridge or freezer; garage; balcony; exterior windows or any window that cannot be reached safely; pest control; rubbish or furniture removal; cleaning of walls or ceilings.

10. The 100% Bond Back Guarantee

10.1 What it is. We back our standard end-of-lease clean with a 100% Bond Back Guarantee. This is a free re-clean guarantee. If your real estate agent or property manager flags a cleaning item that was covered by your booked clean, we will return and re-clean that item free of charge.

10.2 How to claim. To use the guarantee you must:

  • (a) report the issue to us within 72 hours of the completion of your clean;
  • (b) provide the written list or feedback from your agent or property manager identifying the specific cleaning items; and
  • (c) give us reasonable access to return and rectify those items.

10.3 What is covered. Only cleaning items that formed part of your booked and paid scope of work — the standard clean plus any add-ons you paid for.

10.4 What is not covered. The guarantee does not cover items outside your booked scope; areas we could not access or clean at the time, for example because the property was not empty or utilities were disconnected; add-ons you chose not to book; damage or wear that is not a cleaning issue; issues reported more than 72 hours after completion; or any situation where another cleaner has attended the property after us.

10.5 This is not a guarantee that your bond will be returned. Whether your bond is refunded is decided by your agent, property manager or landlord, and depends on factors outside our control — including property damage, repairs, unpaid rent, garden and outdoor condition, and the terms of your lease and condition report. Under the Residential Tenancies Act 2010 (NSW), a tenant must leave the premises in a reasonably clean condition having regard to its condition at the start of the tenancy, fair wear and tear excepted. Our guarantee is limited to re-cleaning the cleaning items we were responsible for. It does not guarantee the return of your bond and does not cover deductions made for non-cleaning reasons.

10.6 The guarantee entitles you to a free re-clean. It does not entitle you to a cash refund in place of a re-clean. See clause 15.

  • (d) show the competitor's ABN and business name;
  • (e) be a genuine, all-inclusive, GST-exclusive total price; and

11.3 What is not matched. Verbal quotes, estimates, or "from" prices; quotes excluding items included in our package; quotes from unregistered individuals or quotes with no ABN; quotes submitted after deposit; coupled or bundled deals; promotional, seasonal or introductory pricing; same-day and short-notice bookings.

11.4 Verification. We may contact the competitor to verify the quote. We may decline a price match if we reasonably believe the quote is not genuine, not comparable, or has been altered. We store competitor quotes you submit for verification and record-keeping. See Part C.

11.5 One claim per booking. The Price Match Guarantee cannot be combined with any other offer or discount.

11.6 We may withdraw or change the Price Match Guarantee at any time, but any change will not affect a price match we have already confirmed with you.
