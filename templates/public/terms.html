1. 100% Bond Back Guarantee Policy

Final Clean provides a comprehensive 72-hour Bond Back Guarantee on all complete end-of-lease cleans. If your property manager or real estate agent reports any clean-related issues during the final exit inspection, we will return to rectify the specified areas at zero extra charge.

Notice Window: Written inspection report or agent feedback must be provided within 72 hours of the clean completion to initiate a free guarantee re-clean.

2. Property Access, Power & Water Requirements

The customer is responsible for ensuring the following conditions on the day of service:

  • Unrestricted access to the property at the scheduled arrival time.
  • Connected and functioning electricity and hot water supply for steam cleaners and equipment.
  • The property must be completely vacated of personal belongings, rubbish, and heavy furniture prior to the cleaning team's arrival unless specifically arranged.

3. Booking, Deposit & Payment Terms

A 10% deposit is required at the time of online booking to lock in your preferred date and time slot. The remaining balance (90%) is due on the day of the cleaning via credit card, EFTPOS, or direct deposit upon satisfactory completion of service.

All prices quoted online include Australian GST and standard equipment and eco-friendly cleaning supplies.

4. Scope of Service & Extra Services

Standard cleans cover all standard tenancy checklist items (bathrooms, kitchen, oven, cupboards inside & out, skirting boards, windows internal, vacuuming, and hard floor mopping).

Specialized treatments such as deep wall washing, external high-pressure balcony washing, ceiling mould treatments, and intensive carpet steam extraction can be added as optional add-ons during online booking.

5. Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations or date adjustments made more than 48 hours prior to the scheduled start time will receive a 100% refund of the deposit. Bookings rescheduled with less than 24 hours notice may incur a $50 rescheduling fee.

6. Contact & Customer Support

For any questions regarding our terms, service warranty, or to request a re-clean under your guarantee, please email hello@finalclean.com.au or call our team directly on 02 7988 9050.
